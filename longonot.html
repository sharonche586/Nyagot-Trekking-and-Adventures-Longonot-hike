🌄 Mt Longonot Hike Itinerary

Nyagot Trekking & Adventures

⏰ Meeting Time: Arrive by 5:30 AM
🚐 Departure: 6:00 AM sharp (No delays)

📍 Pick-Up

Participants along Waiyaki Way can arrange pick-up along the route. We will stop briefly at Shell, Waiyaki Way.

🏞️ Hike Plan

  • Arrive at Mt Longonot National Park
  • Hike up to the crater rim
  • Optional: Walk around the crater rim
  • Enjoy scenic views and group moments

🎒 What to Carry

  • Comfortable hiking shoes
  • Snacks & packed lunch
  • 2–3 litres of water
  • Sunscreen, hat & sunglasses
  • Light jacket
  • Personal medication

🪪 Important

Carry your original ID or passport (required for park entry).

⚠️ Reminders

  • Be punctual
  • Stay with the group
  • Respect nature

🚐 Return

After the hike and rest, we will begin our journey back to Nairobi and arrive in the evening.

Let’s have an amazing adventure! 🥳
